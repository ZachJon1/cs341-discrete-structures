STUDY APPROACH

Make your study time count.

01

Read the definitions.

Make sure you can state each idea precisely before moving into examples.

02

Work through proofs.

Explain every assumption and justify why each conclusion follows.

03

Practice independently.

Try problems without looking at worked solutions, then verify your reasoning.
